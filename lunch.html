Introducing "Jambalaya"

  1. Brown meat and Sauté Veggies to your liking
  2. Add basil, cajun seasoning, diced tomatoes, salt, then pepper. Stir well to combine then add chicken broth and rice. Bring mixture to a gentle boil then add reserved meat to the pan. Reduce heat, cover pan with fitted lid, then cook for 20 minutes.
  3. Best served hot!
