Introducing "Baboy na may kumot"

  1. Mix a cup of water with a cup of water and add a pinch of salt
  2. Mix well until it forms a dough and roll it out into a thin dough
  3. Cut into long triangle shapes
  4. Cook some small hotdogs and brush them with soy sauce and sugar
  5. Wrap the dough in the hotdogs and cook for 5 minutes
  6. Best served hot!
